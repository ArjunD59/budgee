How it works

Three steps, about two minutes. You only need a file from your bank.

  1. 1

    Download your transactions

    Sign in to your bank or card's website, open your account activity, and look for Download or Export. Pick CSV and choose the last 2 to 3 months. More history makes the forecast sharper.

  2. 2

    Drop the file into Budgee

    Drag it into the box below and type your monthly budget. Budgee sorts purchases into categories, spots your regular bills, and ignores transfers and paychecks.

  3. 3

    Read your forecast

    See where your month is headed, how likely you are to go over, how much you can safely spend each day, and the few changes that would make the biggest difference.
